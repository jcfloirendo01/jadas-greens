001 — Our Story

A small farm,
a big crunch.

Jada's Greens started in 2024 in our backyard in Cabuyao, Laguna — a hydroponic setup born out of a simple idea: lettuce should taste like lettuce, and it shouldn't have to travel across the country to reach your salad bowl.

We grow our greens in nutrient-rich water — no soil, no pesticides, no chemicals. Just clean water, sunlight, and a lot of care. Every head is harvested by hand, washed, and delivered the same day to neighbours within Gran Seville.

And yes — that little french bulldog on our logo is real. He supervises every harvest.

004 — How we grow

Soil-free,
fuss-free.

01

Seed

Non-GMO Olmetie seeds nestle into a soil-free plug. The plug holds the seedling steady while roots develop in clean water.

02

Float

Plugs are transferred into our hydroponic channels. Roots dangle in nutrient-rich water that recirculates around the clock — no waste, no runoff.

03

Tend

We check the water's nutrients and pH every day. No pesticides, no chemicals. Just care, sunlight, and clean water doing what they do best.

04

Harvest

Heads are cut by hand the morning of delivery — roots still on so they keep growing in your fridge. Crisp on Tuesday. Crisp on Friday.

05

Deliver

We bring it straight to your gate within Gran Seville Subdivision — free. Outside the subdivision? Just message us; we work it out.
